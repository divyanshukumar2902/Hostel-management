# Smart PG & Hostel Management System

A web-based role-oriented PG and Hostel Management System developed using **Java, Spring Boot, Spring MVC, JPA/Hibernate, MySQL, Bootstrap 5 and Thymeleaf**.

The system centralizes hostel operations including **student management, room allocation, fee and payment management, leave requests, visitor management, notices, mess management and complaint resolution**.

A major feature of the project is its **department-based complaint management system**, where complaints are categorized, prioritized, assigned to appropriate staff, tracked through different statuses, monitored using SLA-based escalation, and verified through student feedback.

### User Roles

* **Warden** – Hostel administration, rooms, students, complaints, leave, visitors and reports
* **Student** – Room, fees, complaints, leave, visitors, notices and mess information
* **Accountant** – Fees, payments and financial reports
* **Complaint Staff** – Assigned complaints, status updates and resolution management

### Key Features

* Role-Based Access Control
* Student & Room Management
* Smart Room Allocation
* Fee & Payment Management
* Complaint Ticket System
* Department-Based Complaint Assignment
* Complaint Priority & Status Tracking
* SLA Monitoring & Escalation
* Student Complaint Feedback
* Leave & Visitor Management
* Notice & Mess Management
* Notifications
* Reports & Dashboard Analytics
* Audit Logs
* Session-Based Authentication with BCrypt
